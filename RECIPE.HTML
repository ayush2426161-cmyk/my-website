Gulam Jamun

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Bread
  • Oil

Recipe

  1. Mix khoya, flour, and a little baking soda to make a soft dough.
  2. Make small balls from the dough and deep-fry them until golden brown.
  3. Prepare sugar syrup with water and add a little cardamom.
  4. Put the fried gulab jamuns in the syrup and let them soak for a few hours.

Jalebi

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • refined flour
  • Oil

Recipe

  1. Mix refined flour with water and make a smooth, thick batter.
  2. Pour the batter into a piping bag and make spiral shapes in hot oil.
  3. Fry the jalebis until they become crispy and golden.
  4. Dip the fried jalebis in warm sugar syrup and serve hot.
